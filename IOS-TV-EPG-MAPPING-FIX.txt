GhostStream EPG mapping fix

- EPG loading is now independent from media-library loading.
- XMLTV channel IDs are matched by exact provider IDs first.
- If provider IDs do not match XMLTV IDs, GhostStream falls back to normalized channel display-name matching.
- Common provider prefixes/suffixes (USA |, HD, FHD, UHD, 4K, 1080p, 720p) are normalized for matching.
- The same resolver is used on iPhone/iPad and Apple TV.
