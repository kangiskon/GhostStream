GhostStream Landscape Performance Fix

This build prevents SwiftUI orientation/layout updates from repeatedly reattaching the video render surface.

Changes:
- MobileVLCKit drawable is assigned only when the backing UIView actually changes.
- AVPlayerLayer player is assigned only when needed.
- Existing buffering and UI throttling remain in place.

This specifically targets playback that is smooth in portrait but becomes sluggish after rotating to landscape.
