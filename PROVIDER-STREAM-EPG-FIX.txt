GhostStream Xcode 26.5 — exact provider Live EPG

Live guide data now comes from the Xtream provider's per-stream short EPG API:
  player_api.php?action=get_short_epg&stream_id=<exact stream id>

Behavior:
- No global guide/channel-name matching is used for Xtream Live EPG.
- Each visible Live channel loads/caches NOW/NEXT data for its exact stream_id.
- The Live player reuses the exact same stream_id guide data.
- Implemented for iPhone/iPad and Apple TV.
- Existing playback, favorites, source auto-load, navigation and VLC fixes are preserved.
