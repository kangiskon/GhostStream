GhostStream Apple TV Media Layout Rebuild

Live TV
- Fixed-width 16:9 channel cards.
- Channel logos use scaled-to-fit so they are not cropped or stretched.
- Titles are clamped to two lines and stay inside the card width.
- Provider category names are cleaned for display and shown in a compact rail.
- Grid adapts to available tvOS width and caps at five cards across.

Movies / Series
- Fixed portrait 2:3 poster cards.
- Poster images fill the portrait card rather than sitting tiny inside a large gray cell.
- Titles are clamped to two lines.
- Primary standard genre appears beneath the title.
- Grid adapts to available tvOS width and caps at six posters across.
- Media links use GhostStream's own presentation instead of the default gray tvOS card surface.

Categories
- Compact category chips with reduced height and spacing.
- Genre caches remain in use, so opening Movies/Series does not reclassify the full library.

No provider, source-fetch, or playback logic was intentionally changed by this UI rebuild.
