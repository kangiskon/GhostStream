GhostStream Xcode 26.6 Compatibility
====================================

This project is marked for Xcode 26.6 / build tools 26.6.
The iOS deployment target remains iOS 16.0.
The tvOS deployment target remains tvOS 16.0.
The iOS target remains universal for iPhone and iPad (device family 1,2).
Bundled MobileVLCKit and TVVLCKit xcframeworks remain self-contained.
Swift language compatibility remains Swift 5.0 with minimal strict concurrency.
