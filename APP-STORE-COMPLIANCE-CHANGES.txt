GhostStream App Store + Saved Services Update

Included changes
- Added Log Out / Switch Service without deleting saved source information.
- Added Saved Services picker for Playlist URL, imported/pasted playlist, and Provider Login sources.
- Provider passwords are stored in the iOS Keychain and removed from UserDefaults during migration.
- Deleting a saved Provider Login removes its Keychain password.
- Logging out disconnects the active source and clears the loaded library/EPG only; saved services remain.
- Renamed user-facing Xtream labels to generic Provider Login wording while retaining compatibility internally.
- Added content-rights acknowledgement when adding a playlist URL, pasted playlist, provider login, imported M3U file, or direct stream.
- Added Settings disclosures explaining GhostStream is a general media player and does not provide/sell/bundle content or subscriptions.
- Added App Store submission notes. No provider credentials or content are bundled.

Before submitting
- Publish a real Privacy Policy and Support page and enter those URLs in App Store Connect.
- Make sure any screenshots/marketing do not imply GhostStream provides copyrighted channels, movies, or subscriptions.
- Give App Review a legal test source/demo if they need one to review playback.
- Confirm licensing/attribution obligations for embedded third-party libraries before distribution.
