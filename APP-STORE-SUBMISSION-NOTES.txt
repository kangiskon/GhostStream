GhostStream - App Store submission notes

Product positioning
- General media player for user-provided, authorized media sources.
- No channels, movies, series, subscriptions, playlists, credentials, or providers are bundled or sold by GhostStream.
- Users must confirm they have permission to access content before saving a new service.

Saved services
- Log Out / Switch Service disconnects the active source without deleting it.
- Playlist URLs, pasted/imported playlists, and provider logins remain in Saved Services until the user explicitly deletes them.
- Do not ship test provider credentials or copyrighted channel artwork in the App Store build.

Before App Store Connect submission
- Replace any placeholder privacy-policy/support URLs in App Store Connect with your published pages.
- Supply App Review with legal test content / demo access if review cannot exercise the player otherwise.
- Confirm third-party framework licensing/attribution requirements for all embedded dependencies.
