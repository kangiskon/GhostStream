GhostStreamTV direct TVVLCKit integration — self-contained

The old VLCKit Swift Package dependency remains removed. TVVLCKit 3.6.0 is now
bundled directly at Frameworks/TVVLCKit.xcframework.

There is no build-time network installer. The Embed TVVLCKit phase selects the
correct tvOS device/simulator framework slice, copies it into the app Frameworks
folder, and signs it for device/archive builds.

No CocoaPods, Terminal install, Swift Package resolution, or VideoLAN download is
required to build this package.
