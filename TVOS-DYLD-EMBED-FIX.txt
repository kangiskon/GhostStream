GhostStreamTV TVVLCKit launch/embedding fix

TVVLCKit 3.6.0 is a dynamic XCFramework. The target keeps @executable_path/Frameworks
in LD_RUNPATH_SEARCH_PATHS and an Embed TVVLCKit phase that copies the correct
bundled slice into GhostStreamTV.app/Frameworks and signs it for device builds.

The framework itself is bundled under Frameworks/TVVLCKit.xcframework, so no
first-build download is required.
