GhostStream Movies + Series fix

- Apple TV now filters Movies and Series using the provider's category_id values.
- Added All/category focus strip to both tvOS libraries.
- Movie and Series artwork URLs are normalized for absolute, protocol-relative,
  provider-relative, and space-containing URLs.
- Added alternate poster fields (stream_icon, movie_image, cover_big, cover).
- VOD and episode direct_source is decoded and preferred when supplied by provider.
- Constructed /movie/ and /series/ URLs remain the fallback when direct_source is absent.
- Container extensions are normalized before stream URL generation.
- Applied shared API/model fixes to iPhone/iPad and Apple TV.
