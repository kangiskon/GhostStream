GhostStream Xcode 26.5 - Live Player Mockup UI

The iPhone/iPad Live player has been rebuilt to closely match the approved GhostStream mockup:
- Back button whenever controls are visible
- Live video remains full-frame/aspect-fit to protect sports scoreboards
- full right-side provider EPG panel using exact stream_id short EPG data
- current programme highlighted with purple neon styling
- upcoming programme list
- bottom channel/program information and EPG progress
- Favorite, -10s, Pause/Resume, +10s, LIVE, Channels, Audio, Subtitles and More controls
- controls/guide hide and return on screen tap
- Movies/Series keep their existing VOD playback controls

Provider EPG remains exact-stream based: get_short_epg&stream_id=<stream id>.
