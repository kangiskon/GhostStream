GhostStream Apple TV — Standard Genre Organization

Movies and Series are now grouped into stable GhostStream genres instead of the provider's raw category list.

Genres:
- Action & Adventure
- Comedy
- Drama
- Horror
- Kids & Family
- Documentary
- Sci-Fi & Fantasy
- Thriller & Mystery
- Romance
- Crime
- Animation
- Reality
- Sports
- Music
- Western
- War & History
- Other

Behavior:
- Movies and Series are classified independently.
- Original provider category IDs remain untouched for source integrity.
- Mixed provider categories can appear in more than one standard genre.
- Series can also use plot text to improve classification.
- Empty genre buttons are hidden.
- Each genre button shows a title count.
- Raw provider prefixes/quality labels no longer clutter the Apple TV category rail.
