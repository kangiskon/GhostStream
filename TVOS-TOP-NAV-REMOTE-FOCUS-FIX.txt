GhostStream Apple TV Remote Focus Fix

Issue:
- The APK-style persistent top navigation could not be reliably reached/identified with the Apple TV Remote/controller.
- Home/Movies/TV Shows/Live TV/Sources had no explicit tvOS FocusState/focusSection.
- The custom button style only showed a pressed state, so there was no reliable top-bar focus indicator.

Fix:
- Added explicit TVTopFocus identities.
- Added @FocusState to TVTopNavigation.
- Bound every section button and Sources/gear button to FocusState.
- Marked the top HStack as a tvOS focus section.
- Added a visible purple focus ring/scale/glow for the focused top item.
- Kept the active-section state separate from focus.
- Preserved poster-only focus rings for Movies/TV Shows.
